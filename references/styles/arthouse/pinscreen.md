# Pinscreen (Alexeieff & Parker)

Arthouse animation techniques. Shared parts: `references/styles/shared.md`; index: `references/styles/README.md`.

- **Is:** thousands of pins in a board. Their shadows make the image.
- **Look for:**
  - Soft charcoal-like tones, no lines.
  - Subtle radial dot texture.
  - Dreamlike, low contrast.
- **In code:**
  - A pin grid where each pin's shadow length follows the value.
  - Render the shadows as short blurred streaks away from one light direction.
- **In motion:** slow, shifting tone.
