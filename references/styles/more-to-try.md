# More to try

Shorter notes on looks without a full entry. The index is `references/styles/README.md`.

- **Cutout puppet** (*South Park*, Terry Gilliam): flat paper parts on pivots, jerky pose-to-pose motion. It's
  the simplest rig (`references/motion-craft.md` §2), with construction-paper texture.
- **Sticker / kawaii:** a thick white die-cut border around each character, a soft drop shadow, pastel colors,
  bouncy squash.
- **Neon / synthwave:** glowing tubes (a white core inside colored `'lighter'` halos), a grid floor running to
  the horizon, a gradient sun.
- **Isometric:** true 30° axes or a 2:1 pixel grid; things move only along the axes.
- **Low-poly flat-shaded:** triangles, each filled with one color from its normal against the light.
- **Squigglevision** (*Dr. Katz*): constant line boil on every line, even in holds.

- **Stippling / pointillism:** blue-noise dots with density set by value.
- **Sumi-e ink wash:** wet bleeding strokes with dry-brush tails, lots of empty paper.
- **Marbled paper (ebru / suminagashi):** iterate displacement fields over concentric rings.
- **Stained glass:** thick dark leading, a jewel-tone Voronoi-ish layout, backlight glow.
- **Soviet Constructivism:** red, black and cream, diagonals, photomontage-free geometry, block type.
- **Hand-tinted photographs:** a desaturated base with a few translucent color washes that miss the edges.
- **Blueprints:** a white line on Prussian blue with a grid, dimension lines and a stencil font.
- **Layered paper cutout** (*Wolfwalkers*, Cartoon Saloon): stacked flat shapes with drop shadows between
  layers, and pencil lines left in.
