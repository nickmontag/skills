# Modern TV flat (*Adventure Time*, *Gravity Falls*, *Bluey*, *Steven Universe*)

Studio, TV and motion-graphics looks. Shared parts: `references/styles/shared.md`; index: `references/styles/README.md`.

- **Is:** 2010s–20s TV animation: simple shapes and bold lines, built to be rigged and animated fast.
- **Look for:**
  - Constant-width outlines, heavier on the silhouette; flat fills with little or no shading.
  - Simple geometric construction and big, readable expressions.
  - Backgrounds as flat shapes with a few texture accents, often with no outlines.
  - A bright, limited palette per location.
- **In code:**
  - A rig (`references/motion-craft.md` §2) with swap sets for hands, mouths and eyes.
  - Constant-width, round-joined strokes with the silhouette pass (`references/drawing-in-code.md` §3).
- **In motion:** on twos, acting through strong poses and expression changes rather than constant movement.
  Moving holds keep characters alive (`references/motion-craft.md` §1).
