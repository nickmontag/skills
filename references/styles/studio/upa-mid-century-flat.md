# UPA / mid-century flat (*Gerald McBoing-Boing*, Mary Blair)

Studio, TV and motion-graphics looks. Shared parts: `references/styles/shared.md`; index: `references/styles/README.md`.

- **Is:** 1950s modernist limited animation.
- **Look for:**
  - Geometric characters.
  - Flat color shapes that don't align with the line.
  - Textured backgrounds (dry brush, crayon).
  - Stylised trees and houses as simple shapes.
  - Limited animation: few moving parts and held poses.
- **In code:**
  - Color blobs offset from the outline.
  - Paper-grain overlay.
  - Only the moving limb redraws.
- **In motion:** snap between strong poses and hold them. Animate on twos or threes.
