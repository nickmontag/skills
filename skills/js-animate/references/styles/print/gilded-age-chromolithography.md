# Gilded Age chromolithography (Puck/Judge, 1880–1905)

Print processes. Shared parts: `references/styles/shared.md`; index: `references/styles/README.md`.

- **Is:** flat color stones under a black key stone engraved entirely in line.
- **Look for:** flat red, yellow and blue, overprinted to make flesh, orange and navy. All tone is hatching.
  Cream stock. Gold foil where money shows. Ornate borders, banners and sunbursts.
- **In code:** keep color and line on separate layers that meet only in the composite. Color layers get no
  gradients, only a solid or a crayon-grain tint.
