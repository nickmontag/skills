# Ukiyo-e woodblock

Print processes. Shared parts: `references/styles/shared.md`; index: `references/styles/README.md`.

- **Is:** Edo-era Japanese color woodblock, many blocks, water-based pigment.
- **Look for:**
  - Thin even black outlines and flat color areas.
  - *Bokashi*: color gradients wiped onto the block, especially sky bands at the top.
  - Prussian blue.
  - Wood grain faintly visible in flats.
  - Stylised waves with claw-like foam.
  - Cartouches holding the title.
- **In code:**
  - Flat fills with one linear or radial `bokashi` gradient per block.
  - Very low-contrast grain streaks.
  - Outlines as even-width strokes with slight ink pooling at corners.
- **In motion:** parallax on flat layers, with no shading change.
