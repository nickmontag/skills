# Ligne claire (Hergé, Moebius, Joost Swarte)

Movements and eras. Shared parts: `references/styles/shared.md`; index: `references/styles/README.md`.

- **Look for:**
  - One even line weight everywhere.
  - No hatching; flat bright colors, cast shadows as flat shapes.
  - Realistic detailed backgrounds with cartoony characters.
- **In code:**
  - Constant-width strokes with no taper.
  - Flat fills.
  - Shadows as separate flat darker polygons.
- It's the easiest style to make look clean and the hardest to make look alive. Push the poses.
