# Flat-vector motion graphics (explainers, Kurzgesagt-style, product and data videos)

Studio, TV and motion-graphics looks. Shared parts: `references/styles/shared.md`; index: `references/styles/README.md`.

- **Is:** designed geometric shapes animated with strict easing.
- **Look for:**
  - Geometric shapes and a limited palette, with no outlines or thin uniform ones.
  - Subtle gradients, soft glows, long shadows or a rim-light crescent on the light side.
  - Everything enters and exits with purpose; shapes morph into the next idea; icons draw themselves on.
  - Constant gentle life: floating, orbiting, pulsing.
- **In code:**
  - Trim paths, morphs, stagger, per-letter text and masks (`references/motion-craft.md` §8), and the springs in §1.
  - Crescent rim lights (`references/drawing-in-code.md` §3); glows with `'lighter'`.
  - Characters as simple rigs: pill bodies, stick limbs.
- **In motion:** the motion language in `references/motion-craft.md` §8, with blur on fast moves. Transitions are match
  morphs and shape wipes, not dissolves.
