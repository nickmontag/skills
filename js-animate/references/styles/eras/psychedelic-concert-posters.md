# Psychedelic concert posters (Wes Wilson, Victor Moscoso, 1966–68)

Movements and eras. Shared parts: `references/styles/shared.md`; index: `references/styles/README.md`.

- **Look for:**
  - Lettering melting to fill a shape.
  - Complementary colors at equal value that vibrate (red on green, orange on blue).
  - Nouveau curves.
- **In code:**
  - Warp text outlines through a displacement field into a target shape.
  - Pick hues 180° apart at the same luminance.
- **In motion:** slow palette cycling makes the vibration pulse.
