# Art Nouveau (Alphonse Mucha, 1890–1910)

Movements and eras. Shared parts: `references/styles/shared.md`; index: `references/styles/README.md`.

- **Look for:**
  - Whiplash curves and flowing hair.
  - A halo circle or mosaic roundel behind the figure.
  - Botanical borders.
  - Muted pastels with gold.
  - Even-weight contour lines.
  - Decorative lettering in panels.
- **In code:**
  - Long Catmull-Rom S-curves with gentle width swell.
  - Symmetric ornamental frame.
  - Flat pastel fills, gold as a metallic gradient.
