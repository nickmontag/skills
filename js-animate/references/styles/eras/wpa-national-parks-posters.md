# WPA / National Parks posters (1930s)

Movements and eras. Shared parts: `references/styles/shared.md`; index: `references/styles/README.md`.

- **Look for:**
  - 4–6 flat screenprinted colors.
  - Atmospheric perspective by stepped value bands.
  - Bold Deco sans lettering at the bottom.
  - Dramatic skies.
- **In code:**
  - Quantise every area to a small palette.
  - Distance equals a lighter, bluer layer.
  - A thin cream border.
