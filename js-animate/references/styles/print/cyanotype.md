# Cyanotype

Print processes. Shared parts: `references/styles/shared.md`; index: `references/styles/README.md`.

- **Is:** a sun-exposed iron-salt print.
- **Look for:**
  - Prussian blue and white only.
  - Objects as soft ghostly contact silhouettes, often botanicals.
  - Brush-stroke edges where the emulsion was painted on.
  - Light areas slightly tinted.
- **In code:**
  - Blue field.
  - White shapes with a 1–3 px blur and a little translucency for thin parts (petals, lace).
  - A ragged emulsion border.
