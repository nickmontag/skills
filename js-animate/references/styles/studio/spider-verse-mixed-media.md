# Spider-Verse mixed media

Studio, TV and motion-graphics looks. Shared parts: `references/styles/shared.md`; index: `references/styles/README.md`.

- **Is:** CG under comic-print treatment.
- **Look for:**
  - Halftone in shadows and chromatic offset on out-of-focus layers, instead of blur.
  - Hand-drawn action lines, onomatopoeia and caption boxes.
  - Characters animated on twos while the camera moves on ones.
- **In code:**
  - Per-depth chromatic split instead of depth-of-field.
  - Ben-Day in the shade.
  - Kirby dots for energy.
- **In motion:** mixed frame rates are the signature. Hold character poses for 2 frames while the camera
  keeps moving every frame.
